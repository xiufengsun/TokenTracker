# TokenTracker Cloud positioning

The Owner selected **TokenTracker Cloud（云服务）** on 2026-10-10 and authorized public release. The product remains a free, MIT-licensed, local-first usage tracker. Cloud is an optional officially hosted service for synchronization, combined device usage and retained personal history. It is not a professional feature tier.

Lead with the benefit and free boundary: “Free, open-source usage tracking. Optional hosted Cloud brings your devices together.” Explain sustainability in the FAQ: hosted synchronization, storage, bandwidth, backups and maintenance require ongoing resources as usage grows; subscriptions support this service and its continued maintenance. Do not claim verified infrastructure growth figures, cost-only pricing, nonprofit status or that only the second device is charged.

Local installations and full local features remain unlimited and free. Free community upload covers one registered device with a daily bounded batch. Personal hosted Cloud requires an eligible trial, transition, gift or paid term, including for one device. Cloud members (paid, trial, gifted and transition) have a 99-device safety cap against abuse, changed usage every 15 minutes while online/running, 90 days of hourly details and 24 months of daily summaries. Do not advertise this safety cap as a selling feature or a routine plan quota. Only usage metrics synchronize.

Display names change across the dashboard, five locales, public pricing/terms/privacy pages, guides, announcement draft and the four existing Waffo products. Internal `pro` fields, translation keys, product IDs, SKUs and historical receipts remain compatible. Prices stay USD4.99/month and USD39.99/year before tax, equal for recurring and fixed-term modes. The launch and transition timestamps do not restart. The unused v1.3.2 draft/tag remains pinned; changed binaries shipped as v1.3.3.

Publication authorization does not create missing evidence: remaining yearly/recurring live transactions are deferred under the Owner's no-new-charges instruction. Bank/wallet refund receipt, first merchant payout and manual Windows installation/login/tray/protocol/export acceptance remain unverified. Archive/deletion schedules stay disabled.
