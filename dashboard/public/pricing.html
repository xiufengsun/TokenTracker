Free local use

TokenTracker Cloud

Official hosted synchronization, storage, bandwidth, backups and maintenance require ongoing resources as usage grows. Cloud subscriptions support this service and its continued maintenance. The complete local app stays free and open source.

Cloud combines usage across computers in a personal account with web access, hosted history and exports, and synchronization/device management for up to five registered devices. Cloud history covers 90 days of hourly detail and 24 months of daily summaries. Only usage metrics are synchronized. Prompts, responses, code, project/session records and AI provider credentials are not uploaded.
